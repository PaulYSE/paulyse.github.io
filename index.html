01 — ABOUT

Who I Am

I'm Paul Yong (杨绍恩), a Robotics Systems Engineer based in Singapore with a strong belief that robotics can meaningfully improve the quality of human life.

I graduated with Honours (Distinction) from the Singapore Institute of Technology, completing a 12-month Integrated Work Study Programme at Yaskawa Asia Pacific — where I worked across industrial robot arms, servo drive systems, and client-facing field engineering.

My project experience spans autonomous navigation, computer vision, fleet management systems, and NLP-driven robot control. I'm comfortable across the full hardware-software stack — from configuring servo drives and PLC controllers to building ROS2 architectures and training ML models.

My programming foundation was built on DigiPen Institute of Technology's HLP1 and HLP2 course materials (High-Level Programming in C and C++), taught by DigiPen lecturer Prasanna Ghali. Although my SIT programme has no formal DigiPen affiliation, my programme lead — who previously managed a DigiPen joint degree — leveraged his connections to bring these materials and the lecturer directly into our curriculum. It gave me a rigorous, industry-calibre grounding in systems programming from day one.

Before engineering, I served as a Section Commander in the SAF Combat Engineers, leading teams under high-pressure operational conditions — an experience that shaped how I approach problem-solving and teamwork in complex environments.
